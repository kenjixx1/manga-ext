Comix Phone Skin

comix.to dressed up to look and work like Tachimanga: your follows as a Library with unread badges, and the same 5 tabs at the bottom.

  • Every screen is built from pages comix.to already has. The script only hides, rearranges and restyles them, and adds links between them.
  • It runs in Safari through the Userscripts app, not in the Home Screen app (an Apple limit).
Rough sketch to explain the idea. Not a final design. The left phone is your real Tachimanga screenshot; striped covers are drawn stand-ins.

Limits

What can't match Tachimanga

Safari tabs onlyNot the Home Screen app. Apple doesn't run extensions there.
No offline downloadsTachimanga can save chapters. A website skin can't.
Unread is an estimateThe badge uses Comix's "read / latest" numbers, so it's only as right as Comix's own progress.
One site onlyThis is comix.to only, not a reader for many sites.
Can break on a redesignIf Comix renames page parts, the skin stops applying and the page looks normal again until I update it.
